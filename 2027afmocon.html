AFMOCON 2027

A gathering with the child at its center.

African Montessori Hub invites educators, leaders, and partners to gather around a simple but demanding question: What shifts when the child's dignity, voice, and brilliance truly lead?

Across many contexts, education systems have been shaped by control, standardization, and inherited hierarchies that often silence the very learners they aim to serve. “The Child at the Center” challenges this paradigm by placing identity, agency, and belonging at the heart of learning, drawing from Montessori principles and Afrocentric ways of knowing to explore how environments can nurture independence, cultural rootedness, and purpose in every child.

“The Child at the Center : Power, Identity, and Possibility.”
Conference theme

Three lenses. One center.

The 2027 theme invites contributors and participants to explore what becomes visible when learning begins with the child rather than the system.

01 / POWER

POWER

How do learning environments recognize the child’s dignity, voice and agency as a foundation?

02 / IDENTITY

IDENTITY

How do culture, place, language and belonging shape how children understand themselves and the worlds they inhabit?

03 / POSSIBILITY

POSSIBILITY

What becomes possible when education is child-centered, culturally grounded and open to new ways of knowing and doing?

What we are looking for

Work rooted in children, practice and context.

Strong proposals should connect the conference theme to lived educational environments and meaningful participation.

01

Child-centered

Proposals that center the child’s dignity, voice and agency in learning environments.

02

Grounded in practice

Work drawing on Montessori pedagogy, play-based and Afrocentric ways of knowing, or both.

03

Context-diverse

Contributions from rural, urban, formal and non-formal learning contexts.
