Welcome to the African Montessori Hub

Cultivating Global Montessori Connections.

The African Montessori Hub (AMH) is a continent-rooted initiative designed to support the growth of Montessori education, honoring and reflecting the cultural identities, languages, and lived experiences of African children and families. Grounded in the belief that education should be liberatory, joyful, and community-driven, the Hub exists to expand access to Montessori for underserved populations while reimagining what Montessori looks like through an African lens.

Our global community

A global collaboration
rooted in local wisdom.

Meaningful change grows through relationships. We work with partners who share our belief in the potential of every child and the knowledge held within every community.

Across classrooms, homes and generations, these connections bring new perspectives to Montessori while keeping African identities, cultures and lived experiences at the heart of our work.

Different experiences. Shared possibility.

Meet our partners

Denver, USA · Community learning

Montessori on Wheels

A nonprofit bringing culturally affirming, hands-on Montessori learning directly to communities. Its mobile classrooms reach children and families across Denver, with a focus on Black, Indigenous and People of Color communities historically excluded from early learning opportunities.

Through family learning, targeted intervention, training and mentorship, MoW supports parents and caregivers as educators in their own homes. Its community approach is an important part of AMH’s work to make Montessori accessible and relevant to everyday life.

Hartford, USA · Learning across generations

NIMBLE

A community partnership initiative of the Center for Montessori Studies, University of Hartford.

NIMBLE brings Montessori into community life across generations. Its work centres on human development throughout life, cultural knowledge and a sense of belonging, connecting local community coaches with shared learning and reflection.

Through the Zimbabwe community partnership, learners explore how Montessori can support their own growth and the growth of others, then apply that understanding in ways that respond to their communities.

Explore NIMBLE ↗

Denver, USA · Ideas into practice

The Audacity Lab

A nonprofit that works alongside communities, researchers and partners to test practical responses to complex challenges. The Lab explores belonging, leadership and systems change through experimentation, reflection and learning together.

Growing from its Up With Africa initiative, its approach recognises local people as the experts in their own lives. That commitment to community knowledge connects closely with AMH’s belief that meaningful education begins with the people and places it serves.

Explore The Audacity Lab ↗

Antananarivo, Madagascar · School community

Bricks Montessori School

A Montessori micro-school in Madagascar’s capital, where children’s independence, curiosity and joy in learning guide the experience. Prepared environments, hands-on materials and individual attention allow children to learn at their own pace.

The school describes a bilingual French-English environment with an appreciation of Malagasy culture. Its focus on close relationships with families and respect for each child brings a distinct local perspective to our shared Montessori community.
